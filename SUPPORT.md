# Support

## Free, in public

Use GitHub Discussions and issues for:

- bugs in this repository,
- questions about self-hosting that the docs do not answer,
- connector and skill proposals.

Read [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) before posting anything that might be a vulnerability. Do not paste API keys, customer data or production logs into an issue.

Response time on free support is not guaranteed.

## Paid

These are not GitHub issues:

- designing skills, workflows or a cockpit for your company,
- a hosted workspace and AI credits,
- an SLA, SSO, or a commercial licence.

How to ask: [COMMERCIAL.md](COMMERCIAL.md).

## Self-host expectations

You operate your database, your Cloudflare account and your model key. The project documents the install. It does not remote in to fix a self-hosted production system unless that is part of a paid services contract.
