# Contributor licence agreement

Status: **process note, not yet a binding agreement.** Have a lawyer review the agreement text before the repository accepts external contributions. Do not treat this file as legal advice.

## Why it exists

The code is AGPL-3.0. The maintainer also sells a commercial licence and runs a private cloud package ([COMMERCIAL.md](../COMMERCIAL.md)). A contributor licence agreement lets the maintainer use contributions for those offers while the public repo stays AGPL.

Without it, the maintainer cannot honestly sell an AGPL exemption.

## What contributors will be asked to agree

Before this is enabled, the expected terms are the industry-standard ones:

- You grant the maintainer a copyright licence to use your contribution, including in a commercial licence and in the private cloud package.
- You grant a patent licence for the contribution.
- You confirm the contribution is yours to give.
- The public distribution remains AGPL-3.0. The agreement does not change the licence of the public repo.
- You are not obliged to provide support.

The likely mechanism is CLA Assistant on GitHub pull requests, pointed at a fixed agreement version stored in this file's history.

## Until the agreement is live

Pull requests are licensed under AGPL-3.0 by the act of submitting them, as stated in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md). The maintainer should not merge external patches that would be needed for the commercial licence until the CLA gate is on.
